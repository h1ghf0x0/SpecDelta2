# Benchmarks Corpus Attribution & Fair-Use Notice

This directory contains 10 publicly published institutional construction Project Manuals and Master Specifications used solely as an automated, reproducible software benchmark corpus for **SpecDelta**.

All documents were downloaded from public `.gov`, `.edu`, and municipal procurement portals and remain the copyright and intellectual property of their respective public agencies, universities, and authoring architecture/engineering firms. They are included solely for non-commercial parser verification, reproducibility testing, and regression analysis under fair use.
